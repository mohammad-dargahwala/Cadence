# Painted Valley

`painted-valley.png` was created with the built-in image generation tool for this project. The app layers moving mist, light and river highlights over it. A procedural canvas landscape remains available if the asset cannot load.

Prompt: Beautiful fine-art oil painting of an immense green mountain valley, enclosing lavender slate and blue-grey mountains on both sides, a winding pale jade river receding from the lower center toward a distant sunlit valley, atmospheric mountain ridges, warm cream and peach clouded sky. View from a meadow slope into the valley. Dreamlike Gaussian-soft edges and hazy depth, rich tactile oil brushwork and palette-knife strokes, impressionist dappled sage and olive fields, hints of ochre and dusty mauve wildflowers at the foreground edges. Refined muted harmonious color, softly luminous late-afternoon light. Expansive and peaceful, artistically sophisticated, full bleed landscape. Tall mountains frame the left and right, open receding valley near the center, river visible in the lower half, calm middle for a timer overlay. No people, buildings, UI, clock, circles, text, symbols, watermark or logos. Avoid flat vector polygons, flat gradients, cartoon illustration, photorealism, hard triangle mountains, synthetic 3D.
